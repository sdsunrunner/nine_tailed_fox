## 案例 1：赛博朋克角色出场

对应素材：`character-concept/001-赛博朋克女战士`（风格参考，不用图片，用文字描述同类风格）

```
Cyberpunk alley at night, neon reflections on wet concrete.
A female warrior with cyan cybernetic implants walks toward camera.

0-4s: slow dolly-in from wide, rain and neon bokeh.
4-8s: mid shot, she stops and glances up, trench coat flicked by wind.
8-12s: close-up, eyes ignite with cyan light.

Rain ambience stereo-wide. Distant hovercraft. Metallic footsteps.
Low synth drone swells on the eye-ignite beat.
No subtitles, no watermark, realistic CG film look.
```

## 案例 2：科技产品发布会

对应素材：`brand-poster/002-科技产品发布会海报`、`event-poster/001-科技峰会海报`（风格方向）

```
A tech product launch on a dark stage with giant LED screens.
Holographic UI panels float above a sleek device on a pedestal.

0-3s: wide establishing shot, audience silhouettes, blue-purple stage light.
3-7s: dolly toward the device, hologram elements rotate around it.
7-12s: camera orbits the device, reflection sweeps across its surface.

Sub bass drone, light whoosh on hologram spin, subtle crowd murmur.
The text "NEXT GEN" appears centered at 3s, holds to the end.
```
